Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 30, 2026

Overview

Elitr ("Elitr," "we," "our," or "us") operates the travel intelligence platform available at app.elitr.ai and the marketing website at elitr.ai. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share information about you when you use our services.

Elitr is built on the principle that your data should work for you — not against you. We collect only what is necessary to deliver Sol's intelligence, and we do not sell your personal information.

What we collect

Information We Collect

We collect information in two contexts: when you visit the Elitr marketing site, and when you use the Elitr app.

Data type
How and why we collect it
Email address
Collected when you create an account. Used for account authentication, transactional emails, and platform communications. Your account email is also the address the app recognises when you forward a loyalty statement or a booking confirmation to your Sol inbox (see Emails you send to Sol, below). We do not use your email for marketing without your consent.
Financial account data
Connected via Plaid when you link a financial institution. Includes account balances, transaction history, and institution identifiers. Used exclusively to power Sol's portfolio intelligence — understanding your spending patterns, card utilization, and earning opportunities. We do not store raw transaction data beyond what is necessary to generate and refresh Sol's context.
Loyalty program and card data
Entered by you. You add your loyalty programs, balances, elite status, expiry dates and credit cards to your Vault directly, or by forwarding a program statement to your Sol inbox, from which Elitr extracts the program, balance and dates. Elitr does not connect to loyalty programs and never holds your program credentials; the Sign in control in the Vault opens the program's own website and nothing from that session is read back by Elitr. Used to give Sol an accurate picture of your rewards portfolio and to surface time-sensitive expiry, status and redemption opportunities.
Trips, confirmations and notes
Booking confirmations you forward to your Sol inbox, trips you build with Sol, and notes and attachments you add to a trip. Used to build your travel brief and timeline and to watch flights on your itinerary.
Calendar events (optional)
If you connect your Google Calendar in the Vault, Elitr reads your calendar events (read-only) to place trips on your timeline. You can disconnect it at any time in the Vault, after which Elitr stops reading it.
Travel preferences and trip context
Information you provide directly to Sol — destinations, travel dates, cabin preferences, hotel preferences, and other context you share in conversation. Used to personalize Sol's recommendations and to build your memory brief, which persists between sessions to improve Sol's understanding of your goals over time.
Conversation data
Messages and context you share with Sol are processed by Anthropic's API to generate responses. This data is transmitted to Anthropic for inference. Please review Anthropic's privacy policy for information on how they handle this data.
Membership and billing status
When you start a membership, Elitr records the status of your subscription (for example trialing, active, canceled), your plan, and the date your current period ends, as reported to us by our payment provider, Creem. Elitr does not receive or store your card number, billing address or tax details; those are entered on Creem's checkout page and held by Creem.
Usage data
Standard technical information including device type, operating system, and interaction patterns within the app. On the marketing site, Google Analytics records page views and clicks on the trial buttons. Used to diagnose issues, improve the platform, and understand how features are used. We do not build advertising profiles from this data.

Use of data

How We Use Your Information

All data collected is used to deliver, improve, and secure the Elitr platform. Specifically:

To power Sol. Your financial data, loyalty balances, travel preferences, and conversation history are assembled into Sol's context window so she can reason about your actual situation — not a generic traveler's situation. This context is updated periodically and stored in your memory brief.

To authenticate your account. Your email address is used to issue magic links and one-time sign-in codes. We do not store passwords.

To send transactional communications. We send emails related to your account activity — sign-in codes, access confirmations, and platform notifications. Receipts and billing notices for your membership are sent by Creem on our behalf. We do not send marketing emails without your explicit consent.

To improve the platform. Aggregated, anonymized usage patterns help us understand what is working and what needs improvement. We do not use individual user data for this purpose without anonymization.

We do not sell your data. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for any purpose, including advertising.

Your Sol inbox

Emails You Send to Sol

Each account has a Sol inbox address containing a code unique to your account; treat it as you would a private address. When you forward an email to it — a loyalty program statement or a booking confirmation — Elitr receives the email through our email provider (Resend), reads its text with Anthropic's API to extract the relevant program, balance, reservation or flight details, and stores what it extracted in your Vault or your trips. Attachments are not read.

Billing

Payments and Membership

Elitr membership is sold through Creem, which acts as the merchant of record. When you start a trial or subscribe, you are taken to a checkout page operated by Creem. Your payment card, billing address and tax location are collected and stored by Creem, not by Elitr, and are subject to Creem's privacy policy. Creem calculates and collects any applicable sales tax or VAT, issues your receipts, and provides the customer portal through which you can update your card or cancel. Elitr receives from Creem only what it needs to grant access: your subscription's status, plan, period end and a customer reference. Cancelling your membership does not delete your Elitr account or the data in it; see Data Retention.

Third-party services

Data Processors and Partners

Elitr uses the following third-party services to deliver the platform. Each receives only the data necessary for their specific function.

Plaid
Financial data connectivity. Plaid facilitates secure connections to your financial institutions and transmits account and transaction data to Elitr. Plaid's use of your data is governed by their Privacy Policy at plaid.com/legal.
Creem
Payments and merchant of record. Creem operates checkout, holds your payment details, collects tax, issues receipts and runs the customer portal. Creem's data practices are described at creem.io/privacy.
Anthropic
AI inference. Sol's responses are generated by Anthropic's Claude API. Conversation content — including messages you send to Sol, the context assembled from your portfolio data, and the text of emails you forward to your Sol inbox — is transmitted to Anthropic for processing. Elitr also uses Anthropic's web search to monitor loyalty-program news; those searches are Elitr's, not yours, and contain no member data. Anthropic's data practices are described at anthropic.com/privacy.
AwardTool
Award availability. When Sol checks award flight or hotel availability for you, the route or property, dates and cabin are transmitted to AwardTool, together with the names of the loyalty programs in your Vault so the right programs are searched. Balances are not transmitted.
SerpAPI
Cash fares and hotel rates. Route or property, dates and party size are transmitted to SerpAPI to retrieve published prices.
OAG and FlightAware
Flight status. The flight numbers and dates of flights on your itinerary are transmitted to OAG (and, as an alternate, FlightAware) to retrieve live status, gates and delays.
Google
Places and Calendar. Place names and your hotel's address are transmitted to Google Places to compute distances on your timeline. If you connect Google Calendar in the Vault, Elitr reads your events through Google's API under a read-only permission you can revoke at any time. Google's data practices are described at policies.google.com/privacy.
Brandfetch
Card-issuer logos. The Vault requests an issuer's logo by the issuer's name; no member data is transmitted.
Resend
Email delivery, outbound and inbound. Your email address is transmitted to Resend to deliver sign-in codes and account communications, and emails you forward to your Sol inbox are received and held by Resend and their text is passed to Elitr. Resend does not use this information for any purpose beyond delivery; Resend's data practices are described at resend.com/legal/privacy-policy.
Upstash
Application storage. Your session tokens, memory brief, Vault, trips and notes, Radar watches and membership status are stored in Upstash's Redis-compatible key-value store, keyed to your authenticated email address. Data is stored in the United States.
Cloudflare
Attachment storage. Files you attach to a trip note are stored with Cloudflare.
Vercel
Hosting and serverless functions. Elitr's front end and API functions are hosted on Vercel's infrastructure. Standard server logs may capture IP addresses and request metadata, and Elitr's own diagnostic logs may reference your account email. Vercel's data practices are described at vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.
Google Analytics
Site analytics, on elitr.ai only. Records page views and trial-button clicks using standard Google Analytics identifiers. Not used in the app.

Artificial intelligence

AI-Generated Content

Sol's responses — including recommendations, alerts, analysis, and conversational replies — are generated by artificial intelligence. Specifically, Sol is powered by Anthropic's Claude API.

Sol's intelligence compounds over time through your memory brief, which stores context about your portfolio, preferences, and travel goals between sessions. This memory is assembled by Elitr and injected into Sol's context with each conversation.

Sol does not make financial decisions on your behalf. All of Sol's outputs are informational and intended to support your own decision-making. Elitr does not provide financial, tax, or legal advice, and Sol's outputs should not be relied on as such.

Retention and deletion

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Specifically:

Session tokens expire after a short inactivity window and are automatically purged from storage.

Your Vault, trips, notes, memory brief and conversation history persist for the life of your account, so that Sol's understanding of your situation compounds rather than resets. You may delete individual programs, cards, trips and notes in the app at any time, and you may request deletion of your memory brief by contacting us.

Financial data is retained only as long as your Plaid connection is active. If you disconnect Plaid, we delete the associated data from our systems within 30 days.

Membership status is retained after a subscription ends, so that the app can tell you when your membership ended and let you resume it. Card and billing details are never held by Elitr.

Account deletion. If you request account deletion, we will remove your data from our active systems within 30 days. Residual data in backup systems will be purged on the next scheduled backup cycle.

Your Rights and Choices

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.
